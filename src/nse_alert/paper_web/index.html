Charges model & practice rules

Illustrative NSE intraday model: brokerage min(₹20, 0.03% of turnover) per filled order; STT 0.025% on sells; exchange 0.00307%; SEBI 0.0001%; IPFT ₹0.01/crore; GST 18% of brokerage + exchange + SEBI + IPFT; stamp 0.003% on buys. Estimates use per-order rounding and exclude special fees and income tax. Verify rates before relying on results. Slippage is configurable. Session net P&L includes marked-to-market open P&L, estimated exit charges, and paid charges. Completed days use realized results. No-trade days pause the streak; zero or negative traded days reset it. Live-price days finalize after the configured square-off time. Overdue positions disqualify the affected days. Mock practice is labelled separately. Seven winning sessions alone do not establish a profitable strategy.
